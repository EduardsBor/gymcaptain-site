GymCaptain

Workout programs and a training log for the gym and home — for beginners and experienced lifters.

  • Programs for muscle gain, strength, fat loss or general fitness, 2–5 workouts a week.
  • Train in the gym or at home with dumbbells, a bench and a resistance band.
  • A video demonstration for every exercise.
  • Log your sets and weights — the app suggests the weight for your next workout.
  • Settings for back, knee and shoulder limitations.

Sign-in. Sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email — no password needed. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile photo. We use them only to create your account and recognise you when you sign in. We do not get access to your Google account or other Google data.

Your data. Workouts and settings are stored on your device and in your account so they sync between devices. We do not sell data and there are no ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

GymCaptain is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor.

Программы тренировок и дневник для зала и дома — для новичков и опытных.

  • Программы на массу, силу, похудение или форму, 2–5 тренировок в неделю.
